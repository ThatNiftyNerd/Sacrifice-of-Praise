What is Sacrifice of Praise?

Sacrifice of Praise (SoP) is a live acapella worship concert designed to deliver a spiritually transformative experience rooted in Hebrews 13:15.

This event brings together believers and non-believers alike in a unique atmosphere of pure, undiluted worship – void of instruments, but rich in harmony, truth, and spiritual depth.

SoP 2.0 Movement: Building on the foundation set in the maiden edition, SoP 2.0 is expanding as a widely recognized worship movement across Nigeria and beyond, thereby promoting the fellowship of the Church of Christ.

Our Vision

To win souls, deepen authentic worship, and redefine acapella expression for a new generation.

Leading a generation into Christ through transformative worship, elevating standards of authentic 21st-century Christian worship, and preserving sacred vocal music.

Our Mission

To deliver culturally relevant, biblically grounded acapella worship experiences that unite and inspire.

Curating Christ-centered worship experiences that inspire genuine devotion, foster unity across the body of Christ, and engage diverse audiences without compromising scriptural truth.
