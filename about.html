The Essence

What is SoP 2.0?

Sacrifice of Praise (SoP) is a live acapella worship concert designed to deliver a spiritually transformative experience rooted in Hebrews 13:15.

This event brings together believers and non-believers alike in a unique atmosphere of pure, undiluted worship — void of instruments, but rich in harmony, truth, and spiritual depth.

The SoP 2.0 Movement

Building on the sacred foundation set during the maiden edition, SoP 2.0 is expanding as a widely recognized worship movement across Nigeria and beyond, thereby promoting the fellowship and spiritual vibrancy of the Church of Christ.
