Privacy & Data Protection Notice

Compliant with Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 & EU GDPR

Summary: We respect your privacy. We collect only the minimum information required to reserve your free seat and generate your admission pass for Sacrifice of Praise (SoP) 2.0. We do not use tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data with third-party marketers.

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller for registration records is the Sacrifice of Praise (SoP) 2.0 Organizing Committee (supported by House603 Digital Solutions), located in Lagos, Nigeria.

2. Personal Data We Collect & Purpose

When you voluntarily register for the concert, we collect:

  • Email Address (Compulsory): For digital event pass verification, event reminders, programme updates, and official Sacrifice of Praise communications.
  • Full Name & Congregation/Affiliation: To personalize your digital event pass, manage seating capacity, and coordinate congregational protocol.
  • Phone Number (Preferably WhatsApp - Compulsory): To deliver urgent event logistical updates, venue protocol instructions, and direct WhatsApp reminders prior to the concert.
  • Referral Source & Lagos Residency Status: For anonymous statistical planning and event logistics.
  • Fellowship Expectations (Compulsory): To help our worship and protocol teams prepare effectively for all attendees.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing (NDPA Sec. 25 & GDPR Art. 6)

Your personal data is processed strictly on the basis of your explicit, informed consent (provided via the unticked consent checkbox prior to registration) for the sole purpose of event administration and attendee admission.

4. Data Storage, Security & 90-Day Retention Policy

Registration entries are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections to restricted Google Workspace registry sheets accessible only to authorized event leadership. In accordance with data minimization principles, all personal attendee records are automatically purged within 90 days after the December 12, 2026 event.

5. Zero Tracking Cookies & Local Storage

This website does not deploy advertising pixels, cross-site trackers, or third-party analytics cookies. Temporary browser session storage (`sessionStorage`) is used strictly locally on your device to prevent automated bot spam during form submission and is cleared automatically when you close your browser tab.

6. Your Data Subject Rights & Contact

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or immediate erasure ("right to be forgotten") of your registration record, or to withdraw your consent at any time.
